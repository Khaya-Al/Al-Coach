Why I teach AI

Most people I know have tried an AI tool once, got a mediocre answer and gave up. Others use it for everything and stop thinking for themselves. I want to show a third way: use it as a sharp assistant while you stay in charge of the work.

What I've done so far

I hosted a session in my residence called "How to Use AI for Academics", where I spoke from the student's side alongside lecturers. I've also helped second-year students work out which AI tools suit which parts of their projects.

I also build websites and projects, including one for the DUT IoT Student Society, which is why my lessons come from real use, not slides.

How I teach

In plain language, on your own work. You bring the assignment, the admin pile or the business problem, and we solve it together. You leave with notes you can repeat alone. I'll also tell you honestly when an AI tool is the wrong choice.

Where I'm heading

I'm building toward a career in cloud and network engineering, and AI coaching is how I'm learning to teach what I know. I want to help more students at my university get comfortable with these tools, one session at a time.
